# Recommendation: Custom Apps for read-only reporting

For teams currently maintaining a Lightdash instance purely for read-only,
non-technical reporting, Supersimple's Custom Apps feature is worth a direct
trial today. The provisioning speed and parameterized-filtering functionality
are comparable, and the switching cost is low relative to the maintenance
burden of a self-hosted Lightdash deployment.

Do not extend this recommendation to teams that depend on row-level access
control or a shared metrics layer — the research did not find evidence of
parity there, and this is flagged as the highest-risk gap for a broader
migration.
