# Custom Apps vs. the BI dashboard landscape

Two of three dispatched models returned research; the third failed after
exhausting retries against an upstream 503. The surviving two models agree on
the core provisioning-speed claim and diverge on access control.

Both models independently cite parameterized filtering as functionally
equivalent to Lightdash's dashboard filters, with one model additionally
noting a documented row-level security primitive that the other did not
surface. Neither model found evidence of a governed metrics layer comparable
to Looker's LookML or dbt Semantic Layer integration — this is flagged as a
material gap rather than a minor one, since it is the dimension most likely
to block enterprise adoption.
